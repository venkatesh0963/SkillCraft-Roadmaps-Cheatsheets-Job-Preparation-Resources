Resume Building Guide

Master the art of crafting an ATS-friendly resume that lands interviews.

📞

1. Contact Information

The very top of your resume should make it effortless for recruiters to reach you.

  • Must Include: Full Name, Professional Email, and a reliable Phone Number.
  • Links: Always include your LinkedIn URL and a link to your GitHub or personal Portfolio. Make sure the URLs are clickable.
  • Pro Tip: You do NOT need your full physical address. Listing your City and State (e.g., "San Francisco, CA") is perfectly fine and saves space.
⭐

2. Professional Summary

A brief 2-3 sentence elevator pitch summarizing your career and top strengths.

  • Keep it brief: Avoid massive paragraphs. Hook the reader immediately. Recruiters spend an average of 6 seconds skimming a resume.
  • Focus on value: Highlight what you bring to the company, not just what you want out of the job.
  • Include keywords: Mention your primary job title and core tech stack directly in the summary (e.g., "Detail-oriented Frontend Developer specializing in React and TypeScript").
💼

3. Work Experience

The core of your resume. Prove your impact using the XYZ formula.

  • Reverse Chronological: List your most recent job first.
  • The XYZ Formula: "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]". Focus on measurable impact rather than just listing job duties.
  • Action Verbs: Start every bullet point with a strong action verb (e.g., Developed, Optimized, Architected, Led).
  • Metrics Matter: Use numbers wherever possible. Did you improve load times by 20%? Did you serve 10,000 users? State it clearly!
🚀

4. Projects

Crucial for entry-level developers or those transitioning careers.

  • Quality over Quantity: List 2 to 3 of your best, most complex projects rather than 10 simple tutorials.
  • Tech Stack: Clearly list the technologies, frameworks, and databases you used to build the project.
  • Problem Solving: Explain the problem the project solved and your specific role in building it. Provide links to the live demo and the GitHub repository.
🛠️

5. Skills Section

Tailor this section heavily to pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

  • Group Logically: Categorize your skills (e.g., Languages: JavaScript, Python | Frameworks: React, Node.js | Tools: Git, Docker).
  • Mirror the Job Description: If the job posting asks for specific tools that you know, ensure those exact words appear in your skills section.
  • Hard Skills Focus: Dedicate this section primarily to technical hard skills. Soft skills (like "Teamwork") are better demonstrated through your Work Experience bullet points.
🎓

6. Education & Certifications

Keep it concise unless you are a very recent graduate.

  • Basics: List your Degree, University/College Name, and Graduation Year.
  • GPA: Only include your GPA if it is exceptionally high (e.g., 3.5+) and you graduated recently.
  • Certifications: Include relevant industry certifications (e.g., AWS Certified Developer, Meta Front-End Developer Professional Certificate).
